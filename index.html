About Us

Novelxplin is a digital content subscription and community access platform based in Ulhasnagar, Maharashtra, India. We operate the N-Store — a Telegram-based marketplace where users can purchase memberships to curated private digital communities.

Our platform connects content creators with their audience through a secure, automated subscription system. Members gain exclusive access to premium digital communities covering topics such as education, entertainment, lifestyle, and self-improvement.

Business Category: Digital Subscription Services / Online Community Membership Platform

Trust & Safety

Payment Security

We take the security of your payments seriously. Here's how we protect you:

  • Razorpay Powered: All transactions are processed through Razorpay, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway.
  • No Card Storage: We never store credit/debit card information on our servers.
  • Webhook Verification: All payment confirmations are cryptographically verified using HMAC-SHA256 signatures.
  • Secure Communication: All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Test Mode: During development, test payments are used — no real money is processed until live mode is activated.
✓ Your payment data is 100% handled by Razorpay. Novelxplin only receives a payment confirmation notification.
